{% extends "docs_base.html" %} {% block title %}Dean Docs | AIPCLPMS{% endblock %} {% block docs_kicker %}Dean Guide{% endblock %} {% block docs_title %}Review & Oversight{% endblock %} {% block docs_description %}Step-by-step review workflows, analytics, and department management.{% endblock %} {% block docs_markdown %} # Dean Documentation This guide is for Deans and Department heads. It explains how to review, approve, and oversee curriculum development within your department. ## 1. Dean Dashboard Overview Your dashboard gives you a high-level view of your department's productivity: - **Pending Actions**: Shows all Course Learning Plans (CLPs) recently submitted by teachers. - **Approval Rate**: Visual metrics of your department's progress this semester. - **Faculty Activity**: A summary of who is actively working on their curriculum. Use the left sidebar to navigate between **Dean Dashboard**, **CLPs**, **Faculty List**, and **Analytics**. ## 2. Reviewing a CLP The core of the Dean role is quality assurance. To begin a review: 1. Navigate to the **Dean CLPs** page. 2. Select any CLP with a **Pending** status. 3. Review the tabs: - **Main Content**: Check the course description and weekly roadmap. - **Learning Outcomes**: Validate the CO-PO mapping. - **Assessment Alignment**: Ensure the assessments used are appropriate for the outcomes. > **Tip**: Look for the **AI Influence** score if available, which shows how much of the plan was generated vs. manually written. ## 3. Approval Workflow If a plan meets all institutional standards, you can finalize it: 1. Open the plan and click **Approve**. 2. **Result**: The teacher will be notified immediately, and the status changes to **Approved**. 3. **Downloads**: Approved plans can be exported as official Word documents for academic records. ## 4. Return for Revision If a plan has errors or gaps: 1. Click **Return for Revision**. 2. **Comment Box**: Provide clear, actionable feedback. Explain *what* needs fixing (e.g., "Week 12 assessment is missing"). 3. **Submit**: The teacher receives the feedback and will be able to edit and resubmit the plan. ## 5. Department Analytics AIPCLPMS provides built-in tools for tracking curriculum health: - **Coverage Map**: See if all required Program Outcomes are being covered by the various courses in your department. - **Timeline Progress**: Identify teachers who are behind on their submissions early in the semester. - **Gaps Report**: Identify courses with weak outcome data. ## 6. Faculty Oversight The **Faculty List** screen shows: - How many plans each teacher has in **Draft**, **Pending**, or **Approved** states. - Last login activity. - Direct links to their active course plans for easy collaboration. ## 7. Best Practices for Deans - **Consistancy**: Ensure all teachers in your department follow the same standards for weekly content. - **Specific Feedback**: When returning a plan, specify exactly which week or outcome needs attention to save the teacher time. - **Outcome Verification**: Regularly check that Course Outcomes actually map to the version of Program Outcomes currently approved by the institution. {% endblock %}
